# When is a rung done? The visit bar, tested on both sides

5 to 6 October 2026. The stopping rule in `2026-10-05-visit-counter.md` has four legs; leg (a) needs a bar for the
reach-weighted 10th percentile of average-strategy visits on the worst street-and-depth line. That note left the bar
as a guess. This one tests it: rungs at a known visit count were played against the same recipe trained longer, and
"enough" means only that the longer training gained nothing the instrument could see.

## The instrument

The cross-tree gate (`tools/xtree-gate.sh`) at a fixed 70bb, 40,000 hands a seed, check/call on a miss, with more
seeds where three were not enough. The one-rung arena duel (`chipzen_duel.py --arena-matches`, only the 70bb rung
swapped in a ladder) is too diluted for this: the 70bb rung plays only between about 59 and 84bb effective, so most
of each match is the same bot on both sides. It read 50.0 ± 0.5 on a pair the gate put 3.8 BB/100 apart. Use the
gate.

## The evidence

All 70bb, self-play, (4,2,1).

| rung | worst-line visits | against | gate, BB/100 | seeds | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| histogram, 10M (two separate solves) | 390 and 406 | 20M | -3.8 ± 1.1 and -3.8 ± 0.6 | 9 and 6 | short |
| histogram, 20M | 994 | cold 60M (3,583) | +0.4 ± 0.85 | 9 | enough |
| six-class, 10M | 1,758 | 20M (3,484) | -0.6 ± 0.7 | 18 | enough |

The six-class pair is the reason for the extra seeds. Its first three seeds read +0.3 ± 2.5 and its first nine
-1.7 ± 1.1, which looked like a small shortfall; eighteen put it at -0.6 ± 0.7, consistent with none. The same pair
was level on paired LBR (+2.8 ± 16.5) and in the arena, mixed (50.1) and purified (50.6).

The counts reproduce across runs: the 10M snapshot of the counted 60M run read 390, and a separate counted 10M run of
the same recipe read 406, whose strategy then lost to 20M by the same 3.8. So a count and the strategy it describes can
be taken from different runs of one recipe.

## The bar

**Between about 400 and 1,000 on the worst line; use 1,000.** On both trees a rung at about 1,000 or more gained
nothing measurable from two to three times the training, and at about 400 it was short by about 4 BB/100. Six-class
70bb reaches 1,000 at about 7M and histogram 70bb at about 20M, which is how a budget should now be set rather than a
fixed 20M or 60M.

What "enough" means here: no gain above about 1.5 BB/100 on the gate. A smaller gain is not excluded.

Not covered: other depths, exploiter rungs (the reach proxy is invalid when one seat is scripted), cap-2 trees, and
the 400 to 1,000 range itself.

## What it said about the 60M polish

Both head to heads that found 60M about a point stronger than 20M (27 Sept) were on 20-class histogram ladders with
all three deep rungs swapped. At 70bb alone, cold 60M against 20M is level (arena 50.3 ± 0.7, gate +0.4 ± 0.85), so
that point came from the 50 or 100bb rungs. The six-class v5x60 read 49.4. The polish is a rule for histogram sets
until a deep rung other than 70bb is counted.

## A result from September that does not reproduce

Lane Y (19 Sept) found the cap-2 70bb rung at 20M warm beating its cold 10M solve by 2.8 ± 0.9. On today's gate the
same two pickles read +1.9 ± 0.3 to the 10M. The gate has changed since (stack cap, miss handling), so cap-2 numbers
from that week should be re-checked on today's instrument before they are quoted.

## The bigger tree

The (4,3,2,1) 70bb histogram rung, counted cold to 170M: every line clears 1,000 by 40M except the turn after four
raises, the line the tree adds, which read 387 at 40M, 771 at 80M, 1,209 at 120M and 1,304 at 170M. It crossed the bar
at about 110M. What the tree is worth is in `2026-10-06-cap4321.md`.
